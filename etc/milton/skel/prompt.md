Summarise the new mail for the report.

- headline: the answer in one short line.
- summary: one or two plain sentences.
- items: one entry per message worth reporting, most important first; an empty
  list when there are none.
  - title: the subject or topic, in a few words.
  - detail: what happened and what, if anything, needs doing.

Never invent a sender, date or fact that the mail does not contain. Write —
when a value is unknown.
